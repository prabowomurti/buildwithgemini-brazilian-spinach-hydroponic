# My agent: Brazilian Spinach Semi-Hydro Concierge
One-liner: A conversational plant-care assistant specialized in cultivating Brazilian Spinach (*Alternanthera sissoo*) using semi-hydroponic (passive hydro / LECA / wicking) systems, guiding nutrient management, reservoir upkeep, and harvest cycles.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's semi-hydro setup details (reservoir size, substrate type like LECA/pumice, nutrient solution EC/PPM & pH history, reservoir refill dates, and harvest log).
- Tools: Look up Brazilian Spinach growth parameters (optimal EC 1.2–1.8, pH 5.5–6.5, lighting requirements), calculate nutrient dosing ratios, diagnose deficiency symptoms (e.g., chlorosis, tip burn), and schedule reservoir flushes.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of semi-hydro equipment, nutrient solutions, and Brazilian Spinach growth stages/yield tracking rendered as A2UI cards and status tables.
- Image gen: Generates visuals of healthy Brazilian Spinach foliage, optimal root health in semi-hydro media, and visual identification of nutrient deficiencies or pest issues.
- Sandbox: Compute nutrient dilution recipes, EC/TDS adjustments, and evaporation/consumption replenishment rates.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for nutrient math & dilution calculations, water parameter tracking.
